Welcome

My name is Donovan Reynolds, and I'm excited to join the Sanford Middle School family. I recently completed my master's degree in computer engineering at the University of Central Florida and have 5 years experience teaching both mathematics and introductory programming. I'm passionate about technology, robotics, and helping students discover how problem-solving skills can be applied in the real world. This will be my first year teaching in a public school and I am excited to for the year.

Outside of school, I enjoy playing video and board games, spending time with my dogs, and strolling through gardens. I'm looking forward to meeting students, working with families, and helping create a fun, engaging, and supportive learning environment this year!
